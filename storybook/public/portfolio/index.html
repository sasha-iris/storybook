Method

  • I hand over code, not a picture. 15 prototypes, built on the product's own stylesheet. Open one and click it.
  • The system runs in React, inside a product built on MUI. 56 wrappers. 150 KB of CSS scoped. 37 MUI components mapped to a replacement.
  • A coding agent does the typing. I write the rules it obeys. 37 mappings, an order of operations, a definition of done. The boundary is in the first paragraph.
  • I measure instead of arguing. Measuring 40 rows killed my own argument in a review.
  • Nothing reaches a stakeholder unreviewed. 48 defects closed before the owner saw the segments work. Then 28 of his 29 points.
  • No invented data. Ever. Records that never ran show no number. Estimates say so on the screen.
