Method

  • Measure, then argue I built both versions of a disputed layout and measured 40 rows. The result killed my own review argument, and the redesign it supported.
  • Adoption is measured, not claimed Every classed element on a prototype is checked against the 616 the library defines: dashboard 80%, P&L 49%, segments 16%. A gap becomes a finding against the library — that is how a 3.0:1 success green got fixed in the token.
  • Three passes before a stakeholder sees it Business analysis, usability and design system, risk-based QA — plus measured contrast. On the segments work: 48 defects closed first.
  • Built in code, AI-assisted Prototypes are HTML on the library's own CSS, not Figma frames — which is why they can be opened, clicked and handed to a developer as they are.
  • No invented data Records that were never calculated show “no data”. Estimates say they are estimates. Prototypes get read as specification.
